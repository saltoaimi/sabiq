# Sabiq Landing Page Design

## Overview

Single-page scroll landing page for Sabiq (سابق), a pre-launch Saudi fintech focused on consumer micro-loans (up to 60K SAR) for auto finance and lease-to-own. The company is in SAMA licensing stage.

**Tagline:** "Driving Saudi Forward"

**Primary audience:** Car dealerships and auto selling platforms (partnership acquisition)
**Secondary audiences:** End consumers, investors/partners

**Tone:** Bold, disruptive, techy, and warm
**Language:** English (Arabic to be added later)

**Primary CTA:** "Partner With Us" — contact form for dealerships and platforms

## Page Structure

### 1. Navbar
- Sabiq logo (left)
- "Partner With Us" button (right)
- Sticky on scroll, minimal

### 2. Hero
- Headline: "Driving Saudi Forward"
- Subheadline: One line explaining Sabiq — digital auto financing and lease-to-own powered by alternative credit scoring
- CTA button: "Partner With Us"
- Clean background — subtle gradient or abstract geometric art, no stock photos

### 3. What We Do
Three cards/columns with icons:
- **Auto Finance** — Quick, digital vehicle financing up to 60K SAR
- **Lease to Own** — Flexible lease-to-own plans for consumers
- **Alternative Scoring** — Digital credit scoring for underserved borrowers

Focus on speed of execution throughout the copy.

### 4. How It Works
Three-step visual flow:
1. **Apply in Minutes** — Fully digital onboarding, no paperwork
2. **Get Approved Fast** — Instant scoring and decision
3. **Drive Away** — Pick your vehicle, we handle the rest

### 5. Partner With Us
- Headline: "Let's Build the Future of Auto Finance Together"
- Short copy targeting dealerships and auto platforms — embed Sabiq's financing at point of sale
- Form fields: Name, Company, Email, Phone, Message (optional)
- Submit button

### 6. Footer
- Sabiq logo + "Driving Saudi Forward" tagline
- Contact email, social media placeholders (LinkedIn, X)
- Legal note: "Sabiq is currently in the licensing stage with SAMA"
- Copyright line
